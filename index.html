Process

How a test works

Four simple steps, and any single item takes just 5 minutes.

  1. Bring your item

    Walk in with your jewellery or coins. No appointment needed for small tests.

  2. Weigh and clean

    We weigh the piece on a calibrated scale and clean the test surface.

  3. Test with you present

    The reading is taken at the counter so you see the result as it appears.

  4. Receive your report

    You get the karat, purity and weight on a printed, signed report.
